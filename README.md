# AI Recruitment Screening Assistant

## Building AI course project

## Summary

The AI Recruitment Screening Assistant would help recruiters compare candidate CVs with job requirements, identify potentially suitable candidates and prepare relevant pre-screening questions. The recruiter would remain responsible for the final decision.

## Background

Recruiters can receive a large number of applications for a single position, making it difficult and time-consuming to review every CV thoroughly.

I chose this idea because I work in Talent Acquisition and screening candidates is part of my everyday work. I have noticed that a candidate may not always explain their experience perfectly on their CV, even though they may still be suitable for the position.

The aim of the project would be to make candidate screening more efficient without removing human judgement from the recruitment process.

## How is it used?

The recruiter would provide the job description and candidate CVs.

The AI system would compare the candidates' experience, qualifications and skills with the requirements of the position.

It could highlight:

* Relevant experience
* Qualifications and skills
* Possible gaps
* Areas requiring clarification
* Questions that should be asked during pre-screening

The recruiter would review this information and make the final decision on whether the candidate should progress.

## Data sources and AI methods

The project could use information from:

* Job descriptions
* Candidate CVs
* Recruitment criteria
* Recruiter assessments
* Previous recruitment information where legally and ethically appropriate

Natural language processing could help the system understand information contained in CVs and job descriptions.

Machine learning could also potentially help identify patterns between job requirements and candidate experience.

## Challenges

One of the biggest challenges would be algorithmic bias. If an AI system learns from biased recruitment decisions, it could repeat those biases.

A CV also doesn't always show the full picture of a candidate. Someone may have transferable skills or experience that has not been clearly explained on their CV.

For this reason, the AI system should not automatically reject candidates or make final hiring decisions.

Candidate privacy would also need to be protected because CVs contain personal information.

## What next?

The project could eventually support more stages of recruitment.

It could help prepare interview questions, generate candidate summaries, track screening outcomes and identify recruitment trends.

The long-term goal would be to create an AI recruitment assistant that handles repetitive administrative work while allowing recruiters to spend more time engaging with candidates and making informed decisions.
